# Cold email playbook
- 50 to 90 words, plain text, no links in email 1, no calendar link in email 1.
- Subject: 2 to 5 words, lowercase, like a colleague wrote it.
- First line about them (the signal), never about me. One problem, one proof line, one question. Sign with first name only.
- Last small line: Not relevant? Just reply "no" and I won't email again.
- Openers: signal ("Saw X is hiring Y..."), insight ("Most [role]s we talk to are dealing with..."), case (only real proof).
Follow-ups (3 max, stop when they reply): #1 (day 3) float it back up with one question; #2 (day 7) one new insight or mini case, 2 to 3 lines; #3 (day 14) "Last one from me. If timing's off, no problem. Should I check back in a few months?"
